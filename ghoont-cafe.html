The Ghoont experience

Three reasons
to stay longer.

A café is the hour you spend in it. These are the rituals regulars plan their day around.

Daily · 7 – 10 am

Decoction Bar

Watch the brass filter drip. Kaapi is poured from a height between davara and tumbler until it foams, then handed over the counter.

Daily · 4 – 6 pm

Kulhad Hour

Chai in clay kulhads at ₹60, with a rotating plate of bun maska and samosa. The clay adds a faint earthy note you won't get from a mug.

Saturdays · 11 am

Brew Class

Ninety minutes, eight seats. Learn filter kaapi, chai spice ratios and a proper matcha whisk. You leave with a 250 g bag of our blend.
